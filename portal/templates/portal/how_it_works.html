Frequently Asked Questions

What are the lead times for booking a corporate vehicle?

Standard city transfers can be booked with as little as 2 hours notice. For upcountry multi-day trips, specialized executive fleets, or large 30-50 passenger buses, we recommend submitting requests 12 to 24 hours in advance to guarantee specific vehicle matching.

How does billing and payment work for enterprise accounts?

Enterprise corporate accounts are placed on monthly credit lines with 15 or 30-day payment terms. You receive a consolidated bi-weekly or monthly statement with itemized digital trip receipts, passenger names, and project cost-center codes, fully compliant with Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA E-VAT) requirements.

What happens if a vehicle experiences a mechanical breakdown?

Our 24/7 Broker Operations Desk activates an emergency replacement vehicle immediately. Within the Greater Accra Metropolitan Area, a replacement of equal or higher tier is dispatched to your passenger's location within 20 minutes, backed by our SLA guarantee.

Do passengers need to download an app on their smartphones?

No! We understand enterprise passengers (executives, visiting delegates, or employees) value frictionless mobility. Passengers receive an SMS with a lightweight, secure web link showing driver details, vehicle photo, and real-time live map tracking without needing to install anything.

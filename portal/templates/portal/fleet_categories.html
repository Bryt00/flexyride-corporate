Quality Assurance

The FlexyRide 45-Point Vehicle Audit

Every vehicle in our fleet provider network undergoes strict mechanical, cosmetic, and regulatory evaluations before joining and every quarter thereafter.

Mechanical & Brake Systems

  • Brake pad thickness & ABS diagnostics
  • Tire tread depth (minimum 3.5mm)
  • Suspension & steering alignment checks
  • Functional air conditioning & cabin filtration

Safety & Emergency Readiness

  • Calibrated speed governor (max 80 km/h)
  • Serviced fire extinguisher & warning triangles
  • Sealed medical first-aid kit in cabin
  • 3-point functional seatbelts for all seats

Legal & Compliance Documentation

  • Valid Ghana DVLA Roadworthiness Inspection
  • Comprehensive commercial passenger insurance
  • DVLA commercial license & NRSA road transport authorization
  • Maximum fleet age limit: 7 years

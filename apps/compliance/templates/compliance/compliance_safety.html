The Four Pillars of FlexyRide Safety

We combine human vetting, mechanical audits, real-time satellite telemetry, and enterprise data privacy.

Pillar 01

Chauffeur Vetting & Conduct

Drivers are the front line of passenger safety. Every chauffeur in the FlexyRide network undergoes exhaustive screening before ever meeting a corporate traveler.

Interpol Criminal Clearance: Mandatory certificate of good conduct from the Directorate of Interpol & International Relations.
Defensive Driving Certification: Certified by accredited driving institutes for defensive driving and evasive maneuvers.
Zero-Tolerance Substance Policy: Random quarterly alcohol and drug screenings with immediate expulsion for infractions.
Pillar 02

Vehicle Roadworthiness & Insurance

Vehicles must satisfy rigorous mechanical fitness benchmarks and carry full comprehensive commercial passenger liability insurance.

Quarterly 45-Point Physical Audit: Inspections of brakes, tires, steering, safety harnesses, and emergency kits.
Comprehensive Commercial Passenger Liability: Full third-party and passenger injury coverage underwritten by leading insurers.
Mandatory Speed Governors: Hardware-calibrated speed limiters capped at 80 km/h on national highways.
Pillar 03

24/7 Telematics & Security Ops

Our central Security Operations Center monitors every active corporate trip with automated geofence anomaly detection and rapid emergency dispatch.

Continuous Real-Time GPS Tracking: Trip telemetry updated at 5-second intervals with route deviation alerts.
Emergency SOS Protocol: Direct liaison with Ghana Police Service 191/185 and private emergency response partners.
20-Minute Replacement Guarantee: Instant dispatch of equal or higher tier replacement vehicle on any disruption.
Pillar 04

Data Privacy & NDA Protection

Corporate itineraries, passenger identities, and board movements are sensitive assets. FlexyRide applies bank-grade privacy controls.

Legal Driver Non-Disclosure Agreements: All chauffeurs sign binding confidentiality agreements regarding passengers and conversations.
Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843): Fully registered with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) Ghana.
Audit Trail Logging: Every request, status change, and approval is immutably timestamped for corporate internal audit.
